who draws all this

An Italian who has spent nine years drawing Panama

Everything in Kocobelo starts with Serena Rinaudo’s illustrations: the quetzal, the toucan, the hummingbirds, the sloths and the country’s flowers, with a European eye for color.

She also designs KCB Moda con conciencia, her clothing line, which walked Panama Fashion Week in 2024.

before you pay

How it gets to you

  • Shipping across Panama

    Panama City B/. 4.00, outlying areas B/. 6.00, rest of the country B/. 6.50. 2–5 business days.

  • Free pickup

    At the café boutique in Boquete or the Clayton showroom in Panama City.

  • Shipping worldwide

    From Panama with DHL from B/. 32.00. Made-to-order pieces are produced close to your country.

  • Pay your way

    Card (Visa, Mastercard, Clave), Yappy or bank transfer.

  • 14 business days to exchange

    From delivery, unused and in its packaging. Personalized pieces are replaced only if defective.

  • Gift wrapped

    For B/. 6.00, with a card and your message.
